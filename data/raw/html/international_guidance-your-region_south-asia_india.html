Why choose UEL?

UEL is among the UK’s top 40 universities in the Guardian University Guide 2027, and London’s highest-ranked modern university. We’ve also climbed 42 places for career prospects in a single year, because every course we design has one goal: your success after graduation.

  • Affordable London experience: Tuition fees and accommodation costs are lower than at many UK universities, with scholarships available on most courses.
  • IELTS waivers available: We accept a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter as an alternative to IELTS for recent graduates who meet our entry requirements.
  • Traditional campus, urban access: Study in East London on a close-knit campus, with direct Tube links to central London in around 35 minutes.
  • Careers-first learning: Our courses combine academic study with real-world experience, including work placements where available.
  • Placement year options: Selected postgraduate courses include a placement year to help build practical experience and employability.
  • Top-ranked student experience: Ranked #1 in London for student experience in the National Student Survey (NSS) 2024.
  • Recognised teaching quality: Named University of the Year for Teaching Quality in the Times and Sunday Times Good University Guide 2025.

Entry requirements

Undergraduate

Students must have completed one of the following:

  • Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) / Standard 12 / All India Senior School Certificate / Indian School Certificate / Intermediate Pass Certificate with an overall average of 60%.
  • 3 or 4-year Diploma (from a State Board of Technical Education or in an Allied Health subject) with 55% or above. Note: Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) includes AISSCE (All India Senior School Certificate), which is a year 12 award conferred by the CBE/CBSE exam board.

Certification details 

 

ALL INDIA SENIOR SCHOOL CERTIFICATE  (including English Core, English Comm or Functional English)

60% or above

NATIONAL EDUCATION BOARDS (CBSE/CICSE):

60% or above

STATE BOARDS:

(Maharashtra/West Bengal/Karnataka/Delhi/Tamil Nadu/Sikkim/Rajasthan/Madhya Pradesh/ Seven Sisters of India (Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland/Tripura):

60% or above 

Kerala State Board:

65% or above (from 2nd year, i.e. Year 12)

ALL OTHER BOARDS / STATE BOARDS:

  • 70% or above Level 2 Maths (if required):
  • Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSC) Mathematics (Year 12) with 50% or Higher.
  • If Mathematics has not been taken in Year 12, then strong Year 10 Maths results will be acceptable (80%+). Year 12 Statistics and Accountancy will be accepted for RDBS courses with 60%+

LEVEL 3 SUBJECT SPECIFIC (IF REQUIRED):

Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSC) with grade B or 70% in a subject-specific course.

First year of a degree or Diploma in a relevant subject / with relevant modules. Foundation year / International Foundation Programme  

 An aggregate score of over 50% / C1 in Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) / Standard 12 / All India Senior School Certificate / Indian School Certificate / Intermediate Pass Certificate, and a score of 50% in Mathematics. 3 or 4-year Diploma (from a State Board of Technical Education or in an Allied Health subject) with 55% or above

Postgraduate

Students must have at least a 3 year Second Division Honours Bachelor of Arts, Science or Commerce (minimum length 3 years), or BEng, BAgriculture, B Laws, B Medicine, B Dentistry (3 year Bachelor or Arts, Science or Commerce, BTech etc - i.e. not Honours also acceptable) and have scored an average of: 50% or above from the following Universities: Delhi; Mumbai; Pune; Kolkata; Chennai; Anna; Bharatiyar; MSU Baroda; Bangalore 55% or above from all other recognised institutions Universities we are aware of with different grading scales and required score:

  • Gujarat Technological University: CGPA 5.8
  • Amity University: CGPA 5.5
  • Kannur University: B or above. (A = 75 to 89, B+ = 60 to 74, B =50 to 59)
  • Postgraduate awards such as master's, D.Pharm, and MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery) would permit entry to suitable programmes.


ADVANCED STANDING

If students have completed 1 or 2 years of a degree or an Edexcel HND, or another recognised qualification, they may be considered (at the School's discretion) for advanced entry.

PRE-MASTERS

Students must have to pass an aggregate score of 40% (Third Class) degree from a recognised educational body.

Notes:

MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery) programmes do not give a final classification, so a 'pass' would be acceptable.

On some degree certificates, the classification mentions two (or three parts. Part I (or Parts I and II) usually refer to compulsory language studies such as English and Hindi, or a regional language, while Part II (or Part III) refers to the major areas of study.

The standard pattern for a bachelor's degree used to be two years of full-time study following 10 years of schooling and two years of intermediate study.

The two-year bachelor's degree was offered at some universities in West Bengal up to 1999/2000, but it was gradually phased out in the other states starting in the 1960s. The present system is commonly referred to as the 10+2+3 pattern, requiring three years of study for a bachelor's degree in arts, science and commerce. (Source: Nokut)

A provisional degree certificate can be accepted; however, it must be accompanied by final/official transcripts.

English Language Qualifications

Accepted English Language Qualifications:

  • IELTS Academic
  • IELTS for UKVI
  • TOEFL iBT
  • Pearson PTE Academic
  • Trinity College London Integrated Skills in English
  • Cambridge IGCSE

We do not accept:

  • IELTS General
  • TOEIC
  • IGCSE / GCE O Levels in English Literature. Only English Language is accepted.

Learn more about accepted English language qualifications.

English language requirements

Undergraduate

All students are required to meet our standard English Language Requirements, e.g. IELTS, TOEFL IBT or Pearson Test of English (PTE), except for students who have studied in English medium throughout their studies and achieved one of the following results in the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC)

  • All India Senior School Certificate
  • Indian School Certificate
  • Intermediate Pass CertificateHigher Secondary Certificate (HSC)
  • Standard 12
  • All India Senior School Certificate
  • Indian School Certificate
  • Intermediate Pass Certificate

Postgraduate 

To study at UEL you must demonstrate that your English level is at the standard for university study. We accept a number of different English language qualifications/tests for entry onto our courses, with IELTS being the most common.
