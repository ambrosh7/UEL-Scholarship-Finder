Fees

All applicants must provide fee assessment information to determine if they will be paying international tuition fees.

Undergraduate tuition fees

Average length: 3 years

For new full-time international students starting in the 2026-27 academic year: £16,020 per year. 

Postgraduate tuition fees

Course fees for new postgraduate students are provided on our individual postgraduate course pages.

2026-27 fees:

Postgraduate courses (avg. length: 1 year)

Fees: from £16,020 per year (MArch Architecture only)

 

MBA (avg. length: 1 year)

Fees: £19,500 per year (full time)

 

MBA with Placement

Fees: £19,500 (Year 1), £3,500 (Year 2 Placement Fee)

Payment plans are available for international students; for more information, please contact international@uel.ac.uk

International students must pay a tuition fee deposit before a Confirmation of Acceptance number can be issued. The required deposit amount varies by the level of study, and details on how to pay and the specific amount can be found in your Offer Letter/Email.

Read our tuition fees policy for more information.

Funding

Saving money on your fees

You may be eligible to apply for an international scholarship to study at the University of East London. Our International Office can provide guidance on opportunities available in your home country, the UK, or within your specific subject area.

International Scholarships

The University offers scholarships and discounts of up to £5,000 for international, self-funded students who pay full overseas fees. You can view the available scholarships and find out more by contacting the international student recruitment team at international@uel.ac.uk.

Note: this does not apply to US students applying for Federal Aid (federalaid@uel.ac.uk) or those on full external scholarships.

Early Payment Discount

You can receive a 5 per cent discount on your undergraduate or postgraduate tuition fees if you pay them in full before mid-September of your starting year. 

If you are a postgraduate student starting in either September or January, you must pay your tuition in full before the date specified in your Offer Letter/Email to be eligible for the discount.

Alumni Scholarship

If you previously studied at UEL and were awarded a qualification, you may be eligible for our Alumni Scholarship, which offers up to 25  per cent off your tuition fees for subsequent courses.

To apply, you must already hold one of the following qualifications from UEL:

  • undergraduate degree
  • master's degree
  • professional doctorate degree
  • postgraduate research degree

This discount is not applicable to distance learning, post-compulsory education and training (PCET) courses, or any short courses worth less than 120 credits. 

  • Students who progress directly from an undergraduate to a postgraduate programme at UEL with no gap year will receive a 15 per cent tuition fee discount. 

  • The 15 per cent discount for direct progression cannot be combined with the Alumni Scholarship.

Explore our alumni scholarship

Course Specific Scholarships

For some courses such as architecture or in the field of healthcare, there are sometimes scholarships or bursaries available. Check the course details on the website to find out whether this applies to you.

To find out more about course-specific scholarships for international students, email international@uel.ac.uk

Sports Scholarships

With our outstanding SportsDock facilities, UEL attracts talented young athletes with one of the most attractive high-performance sports programmes in the UK. This programme allows elite athletes to balance their academic studies with their sporting ambitions.

What we offer 

The programme provides world-class facilities and comprehensive support, including: 

  • Non-repayable bursaries for international (non-EU) students worth up to £6,000 a year.
  • Up to £1,000 worth of support services, such as free access to SportsDock, free physiotherapy, and sports massages.
  • Up to £1,000 for students with a coaching qualification to cover the cost of additional sports qualifications.

Eligibility 

To be eligible for the programme, you must be seeking to study for a minimum of one year and have already received an offer to study at UEL. Our sports scholars include Olympians, Paralympians, and senior international athletes who represent UEL at national and international university competitions.

. Visit our sports scholarships pages.

TEAMUEL Sports Scholarships

What TeamUEL offers

TeamUEL members receive over £1,000 worth of discounts and support, including: 

  • Free TeamUEL kit
  • Silver SportsDock membership
  • Sports Club membership
  • Club training
  • A ticket to the annual Sports Awards dinner

Additional support 

You can also choose up to £550 in additional support, such as: 

  • Free Gold SportsDock membership
  • Five personal training sessions
  • Meal vouchers
  • A coaching qualification
  • A £250 cash bursary
  • Sports social night vouchers

To be eligible, you need to be accepted onto a course with us. To apply, complete our online TeamUEL application form.

Bursaries

We run a number of schemes which offer financial support to enrolled international students.

  • The Emergency International Fund: This fund is for international students who are facing financial difficulty due to an unforeseen expense, despite having previous funding to cover their living costs.
  • The International Engagement Fund: This fund is available to international students who live outside of London travel zones 1-9 due to high rental costs and need help with additional travel costs for their commute to the university or a required work placement.
  • The International Progression Fund: This fund provides up to a £1,000 award to help international students cover high course-related expenses.

Enrolled students can learn more about international bursaries on the intranet.

FAQs - international fees and funding

How can international students pay their deposit and tuition fees?

The best way to pay your deposit is via Flywire. You will need to log into your applicant portal, click on 'International Checks', then 'deposit/payment'. This will take you through to Flywire, where you can make your payments.

How much are UEL tuition fees for international students?

Fees vary per course, so please check the individual course page.

What scholarships are available for international students at the University of East London?

If eligible (based on your course), it will be indicated and outlined in your Unconditional Offer letter. Scholarships range from £500 to £5,000 off the first year's tuition fees.

What are the eligibility criteria for scholarships for international students?

When you apply for an eligible course and receive an offer, we automatically check whether you qualify for an international scholarship (or discount). If you are eligible, we will include the scholarship amount in your offer letter.

Does the University of East London offer bursaries or financial aid for international students?

No, UEL does not offer full funding, but international students are eligible for the offered International Scholarship.

UEL also provides several bursary schemes to support international students facing financial challenges.

Can I get a discount on my tuition fees if I pay early?

Yes. UEL offers a 5 per cent Early Payment Discount for international undergraduate and postgraduate students who pay their tuition fees in full before mid-September of the intake year (or before the deadline in your Offer Letter for January start dates).

How can I request a refund on my tuition fees?

Go to the 'Contact Us' button in the Applicant Portal, select 'Withdraw/Decline Application', and provide the details. You can then request the refund via the portal.
