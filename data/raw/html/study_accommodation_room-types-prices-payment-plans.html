UEL student accommodation prices for 2026/27 range from £189 per week for a West Halls standard en-suite room to £238 per week for an East Halls self-contained studio. All prices are fully all-inclusive, utilities (gas, electricity, water), high-speed Wi-Fi (up to 100 Mbps), and maintenance support are covered with no additional bills. Over 1,100 students live in our on-campus Docklands Student Village, which offers a range of en-suite room types across East and West Halls.

Why UEL accommodation offers better value than private rentals

All-inclusive rent: utilities, Wi-Fi, and TV licence covered

With weekly rent from £189, you'll know exactly what you're paying each month. Your rent includes gas, electricity, water, high-speed Wi-Fi, a TV licence in eligible flats, 24/7 security and maintenance support, so there are no unexpected bills to budget for.

How UEL prices compare to private East London rents

Finding privately rented accommodation in East London often means paying separate utility bills, internet costs and other household expenses on top of your rent. At UEL, these costs are included in one weekly payment, helping you budget with confidence while living on campus close to your classes and transport links.

Premium accommodation

We offer premium accommodation in both East and West Halls. In East Halls, the premium accommodation is in Longbridge House and in West Halls, it is located in Felix, Kwame, Jamilah and Ailsa Houses. They offer the same en-suite rooms and shared kitchen living as our standard halls; however, the rooms and kitchens have been recently refurbished.

Payment plans and rent information

A student accommodation payment plan is a way of paying your student housing rent by dividing the total amount due into several equal, smaller instalments. These are then paid throughout the year. The total amount that you owe will be your total rent for your tenancy, minus any payments you have already made. Your payment plan will be available for you to set up via UEL Direct within 72 hours of accepting your room offer for the Halls of Residence. You will be emailed as soon as your payment plan task is ready for you to complete.

 

Payment plan options

Your student accommodation payment plan options are dependent on what tenancy type you select:

  • Term 1 only or Term 2 only Tenancy: Option to pay in 3 instalments.
  • Full Academic Year Tenancy (including Nov intake): Option to pay in 3 or 7 instalments.
  • Full Academic Year and Summer Tenancy (including Nov intake): Option to pay in 3, 7, or 10 instalments. 

All tenancy types come with the option to pay for the entire cost of your rent at the start of your stay, in one payment. To do this, set up any payment plan and make a payment for all instalments at once. Instructions on how to make a payment are below. 

 

Setting up your payment plan

Open UEL Direct and enter your UEL email (Example: u1234567@uel.ac.uk). Enter your password, which is your date of birth in the following format: ddMmyyyy (Example: 06Jan1991). Then, click the “Sign-in” button. You will only have access to UEL Direct once your enrolment task is available to you. If you have issues accessing UEL Direct, please contact the Student Hub via 0208 223 4444.

Once you are logged into UEL Direct, go to “My Payments” > “Student Statement” > “Set Up Payment Plan” > select instalment amounts > “Confirm and Submit” > “Exit”.

If you wish to change your payment plan, please email residentiallife@uel.ac.uk. You cannot change your payment plan if you are in arrears.

 

Deadline to set up your payment plan

We ask that all students set up their student accommodation payment plan before their arrival at the Halls of Residence. However, we understand that due to access to UEL Direct and Enrolment, this may not always be possible. We therefore have the following deadlines in place:

  • If you arrive as part of the September intake, your payment plan must be set up by 30 October. Failure to set up your payment plan will result in your full rent being due on 30 October.
  • If you arrive mid-year, after the September intake, you need to ensure your payment plan is set up by the 30th of the month you arrive. For example, if you arrive on 5 November, your payment plan must be set up by 30 November. Failure to set up your payment plan by the 30th of your arrival month will result in your full rent being due on the 30th of that month.

 

How to make a payment

Paying via recurring card payments is no longer an option for paying your rent. Payments need to be paid manually via UEL Direct by going to My Payments > Student Statement > “Make an Accommodation Payment”. You will then be directed to the payment system, Flywire. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about UEL room prices

What is the cheapest room at UEL?

The most affordable room at UEL is the West Halls standard en-suite bedroom, available from £189 per week for the 2026/27 academic year. This includes all utilities and Wi-Fi.

Does UEL accommodation include bills?

Yes. All UEL on-campus accommodation is all-inclusive. Gas, electricity, water, high-speed Wi-Fi (up to 100 Mbps), and maintenance support are all included in the weekly rent.

Can I pay UEL accommodation fees in instalments?

Yes. UEL offers payment plans with 3, 7, or 10 instalment options depending on your tenancy type. Payment plans are set up via UEL Direct. International students can pay via Flywire.

Can I pay for UEL accommodation from outside the UK?

Yes. UEL uses Flywire for international payments, which supports bank transfers in local currencies including Indian Rupees, Nigerian Naira, and Pakistani Rupees.
