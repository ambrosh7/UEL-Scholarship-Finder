### Headline

They are 17, still in Class 12, and the whole family decides. This page is a plan to discuss, not a result that pretends the exams are over. A headline that treated this as admission would be both dishonest and frightening.

### Course and eligibility

Business is what they asked for. The 75% is expected, so we say above the published 60% guide and immediately that it is not a final result. That stops two lies: a fake place, and a panic that they already failed.
